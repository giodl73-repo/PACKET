# PACKET public finding release boundary

PACKET findings are research and conceptual-design evidence. They are not
engineering plans, RF or optical designs, regulatory determinations, funding
instructions, carrier commitments, FCC/NTIA endorsements, standards-body
endorsements, or complete national connectivity findings.

This boundary closes `PACKET-PF-05`.

## Required release evidence

A PACKET finding may be reused outside repo-local research only when the release
record names:

1. finding path and command used to regenerate it;
2. scale, market set, assessed dimensions, and unassessed dimensions;
3. source ids, proxy labels, estimates, and confidence limits;
4. exact public claim allowed and claims explicitly blocked;
5. full parliament review across the seven PACKET voices;
6. editorial review by Citation Auditor, Scope Keeper, and Numeracy Checker; and
7. downstream owner acceptance for any local adaptation, public report, build
   plan, funding, procurement, regulatory, or advocacy use.

If any field is missing, the finding remains a cited research result and local
adaptation starting point only.

## Current first-finding status

`docs/findings/2026-06-broadband-adoption-divide.md` is useful and
reproducible for its cited regional run. It reports a mean-level null result and
a tail gap across ten US state examples. It does not by itself authorize a
